# Budget travel playbook

## 1. Split the budget before you search
- **Transport: 35–40%** of the total (flights there and back, airport transfers).
- **Stay: 35–40%**: search hotels with `maxPricePerNight = stay budget ÷ nights`.
- **The rest (20–30%)** goes to activities, food and local transport.
- Keep **at least 10% unspent** as a buffer. Never plan to the last euro.

## 2. Flights
- **Avoid arrivals after midnight.** A cheap 02:10 landing usually costs more than it saves: public transport has stopped, a taxi is expensive, and you pay for a hotel night you barely use. Say so if you skip a cheaper flight for this reason.
- Count **airport transfers**: secondary airports are far out. Paris Beauvais (BVA) to the city is a shuttle bus, about **€17 each way, 75 min**.
- If a date has no flights, try the next day before changing the whole plan.

## 3. Days and weather
- Put **outdoor** activities on dry days and **indoor** ones on rainy days.
- At most 2 paid activities a day. Leave room for walking around and meals.
- Budget about **€35 a day for food** in Paris (bakery breakfast, set-menu lunch, simple dinner).
- Check opening days (many museums close on Monday or Tuesday).

## 4. Trip plan format
1. A one-line summary: dates, destination, total cost and remaining buffer.
2. Transport: outbound and return, with times and prices.
3. Stay: hotel, area, nights and total.
4. Day by day: the weather, then 2–3 activities with prices.
5. A budget table: transport, stay, activities, food, total, buffer.
